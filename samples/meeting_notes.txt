Connected Vehicle Experience — Q3 Planning Meeting
Attendees: Sarah (VP Connected Experience), Ravi (Director Vehicle Software),
           Kenji (Mobile App Lead), Petra (Fleet & Dealer Tech),
           Yusuf (Compliance), Nalini (Warranty & Service PM)
Date: April 11

Sarah kicked off — said the last 90 days of feedback from fleet operators,
dealer service advisors, and the in-app survey are telling us pretty
consistent things. She wants us to leave this meeting with a synthesized list
of driver-facing problems we should tackle in Q3.

------

Theme 1: OTA update failures during peak deployment windows

Petra led on this. Last month was the worst — three regional deployment batches
affecting vehicles in the Southwest cluster failed to apply during the evening
window. Vehicles sat in "Update Pending" for over 40 minutes, blocking the
next scheduled deployment wave. Owners reported their infotainment was
unresponsive during the stuck state. Petra's data: when the CDN throughput
cap is hit, our OTA orchestrator keeps retrying without backing off — it can't
even fall back gracefully because it can't validate the partial download
locally.

She wants us to build a proper staged-rollout with offline resilience for
the OTA system. At minimum: vehicles should resume normal function
immediately if an update stalls, retries should use exponential back-off,
and the rollout throttle should be configurable per deployment ring.
She thinks auto-rollback would be ideal but compliance is going to push back.
(Yusuf confirmed — ISO 26262 requires a validated rollback path, not an
automatic one for safety-critical ECU updates.)

Ravi asked: "Can we checkpoint the download progress on the vehicle so
when connectivity resumes it picks up where it left off instead of
restarting the full package?" Petra: "Yes, that's the design. Local
staging partition on the TCU, resume from byte offset when online."

Sarah: "This is high priority. We're seeing owner satisfaction drop after
failed update nights and dealers are escalating."

------

Theme 2: Navigation search keeps surfacing unavailable EV charging stations

Kenji has been deep in the feedback. Drivers search for "EV charger"
and the top three results show stations that are offline or at capacity.
They navigate there and arrive to find non-functional chargers or a full
lot. NPS for drivers who hit this is -32. Drivers who don't hit it are +20.

Three things tangled here:
- Charger status data in our POI index is refreshed only every 6 hours
- The "Available Now" badge pulls from a third-party status feed that
  disagrees with real-time network data about 22% of the time
- Alternative charger suggestions don't appear until after the driver
  has already started navigating, way too late

Kenji: "I think we want to push live charger-network telemetry into the
search ranker as a boost factor. And surface in-range alternatives inline
when the top result shows low availability."

Yusuf raised: "We need to make sure we're not violating advertising rules
if we boost sponsored charging networks based on availability — we can't
show differential placement to different users without disclosure."

Sarah: "Note that for legal review. Don't let it block us — find the
non-controversial path."

------

Theme 3: Warranty claim status confusion

Nalini brought receipts. The single biggest complaint to the dealer service
helpline is "I submitted a warranty claim in the app but the service advisor
says they have no record and the part hasn't been ordered." It's because we
have TWO claim intake channels: the in-app one, and the dealer service desk
direct entry. They write to different systems and the systems don't
reconcile in real time.

Nalini wants:
- One unified claim record per VIN/incident, regardless of intake channel
- A clear status the owner sees ("submitted / parts ordered / ready for drop-off")
- A push notification when the repair slot is actually confirmed

Yusuf: "GDPR and CCPA. Any notification that mentions a specific defect
or recall code has to be consent-based and we can only send to the
vehicle owner's verified contact method — not a shared fleet account."

------

Theme 4: Connected services subscription tier confusion

Sarah mentioned this almost as an aside but it kept coming up. Owners don't
understand how they transition between or lose connected service tiers. They
get a "Welcome to Premium Connected!" email at delivery and then 12 months
later "Your trial has ended — you've been moved to Standard" and they're
upset because they didn't realise the premium tier was a trial. The trial
end date isn't visible from any owner-facing surface.

Kenji thinks the fix is mostly informational — show a "subscription status"
card in the app that makes the trial end date, included features, and
renewal path visible. Ravi: "Should be a small UI addition. Not a platform
rewrite. We can fit it in."

------

Theme 5: Legacy Gen1 TCU hardware in 2018–2020 vehicles

Petra: side-bar but worth flagging. Vehicles in the 2018–2020 model years
are running the Gen1 Telematics Control Unit with 512 MB RAM. They can't
receive differential OTA packages — only full firmware images — and they
don't support TLS 1.3. Replacement hardware is approved for the next
service campaign but won't reach full coverage until FY26 Q2.

Ravi: "Good call-out. Flag this on every new connected-feature story —
the Gen1 TCU has a different capability floor than Gen2 vehicles."
