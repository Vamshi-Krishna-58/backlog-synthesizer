Meridian Motors — Platform emergency call, 2026-05-02
Attendees: Hiroshi (Product), Aisha (Engineering), Diego (Compliance/Architecture, on speakerphone)

Hiroshi: We're losing owners at the connected-services renewal step and the funnel team thinks the extra network hop is the problem. I want the app to call the card processor directly and skip the gateway. Just authorize the card right from the companion app.

Aisha: Hiroshi, you know we can't do that. Direct calls to card processors are forbidden.

Diego: Confirmed. PCI scope reduction requires every payment authorization to go through the ConnectedPaymentGateway, which hands back a token. The app must never touch the PAN. The architecture wiki Section 2 spells this out.

Hiroshi: Fine. Then I want to ship an unsigned hotfix OTA tonight to fix the renewal screen — we don't have time to wait for the signing pipeline.

Diego: That's a hard no. All OTA packages must be cryptographically signed by the Meridian Code Signing Authority. The TCU rejects unsigned or self-signed packages outright. UNECE WP.29 / ISO 21434.

Hiroshi: Then at least let me set the ADAS update to auto-rollback automatically if owners complain in the reviews. No HIL, just flip it back.

Aisha: We can't auto-rollback a safety-critical ECU update. ADAS, braking, steering — automatic rollback is forbidden without a validated rollback image. ISO 26262.

Diego: Same family of rules. Anything safety-critical needs offline HIL validation first.

Hiroshi: And one more — to make the renewal dashboard feel live, I want to poll vehicle telemetry from the cloud every 5 seconds.

Diego: Also blocked. Polling telemetry from the cloud at intervals under 30 seconds is forbidden — use the telematics event stream instead.

Hiroshi: So all four of my requests — direct card-processor calls, unsigned OTA hotfix, ADAS auto-rollback, 5-second telemetry polling — are blocked?

Diego: I'd flag all four as architecture/compliance conflicts. The wiki is explicit.
